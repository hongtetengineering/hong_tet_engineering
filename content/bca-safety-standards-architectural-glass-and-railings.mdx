# BCA Safety Standards for Architectural Glass & Stainless-Steel Railings

Whether installing a modern frameless glass entrance in a Raffles Place corporate office or erecting perimeter stainless-steel balustrades for a landed bungalow in Bukit Timah, architectural installations in Singapore must balance modern aesthetics with strict Building and Construction Authority (**BCA**) safety mandates.

Here is an overview of key technical requirements and material specifications applied by **HONG TET ENGINEERING (S) PTE. LTD.**

---

## 1. Grade 304 vs. Grade 316 Marine Stainless Steel

Stainless steel provides timeless beauty and structural strength, but choosing the incorrect alloy grade can lead to unsightly tea-staining and premature surface pitting:

- **Grade 304 Stainless Steel:** Best suited for interior handrails, office staircases, and enclosed building vestibules with controlled humidity.
- **Grade 316 Marine-Grade Stainless Steel:** Essential for all exterior balconies, exposed main gates, and coastal perimeter railings. The addition of **2%–3% molybdenum** provides robust resistance against chlorides and airborne salinity.

---

## 2. Glass Balustrade Engineering Requirements

Under BCA Singapore guidelines, glass balustrades installed in areas with a drop of more than 1 meter must meet stringent impact and horizontal load resistance:

1. **Tempered Laminated Safety Glass:** Typically specified with a minimum buildup of **12mm to 17.52mm** (e.g., 8mm + 1.52mm PVB + 8mm).
2. **Continuous Top Rail or Edge Clamps:** Ensures structural retention so that even if one glass pane fractures, the adjacent panes and continuous handrail maintain barrier protection.
3. **Recessed Base Shoe Channels:** Heavy-duty aluminum or stainless-steel U-channels anchored directly into concrete slabs using certified chemical anchors.
