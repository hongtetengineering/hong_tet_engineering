# Structural Steel & I-Beam Installation in Singapore

Industrial warehouses, logistics hubs, and manufacturing facilities across Singapore—from **Tuas to Jurong and Woodlands**—require robust, high-load structural frameworks capable of supporting heavy overhead cranes, mezzanine platforms, and dynamic warehouse operations.

At **HONG TET ENGINEERING (S) PTE. LTD.**, we specialize in fabricating and erecting certified structural steel I-beams tailored specifically to local Building and Construction Authority (**BCA**) codes and Workplace Safety and Health (**WSH**) regulations.

---

## 1. Selecting the Right Universal Beam (UB) Profiles

When planning an industrial steel frame or mezzanine extension, selecting appropriate steel profiles is critical for structural deflection limits:

- **Universal Beams (UB):** Primarily utilized as horizontal load-carrying beams designed to resist bending forces across expansive column-free spans.
- **Universal Columns (UC):** Square-profile structural sections optimized for axial vertical compression, ideal for multi-tier mezzanine supports.
- **Structural Hollow Sections (SHS / RHS):** Torsionally rigid tubular profiles frequently deployed in secondary canopy purlins and bracing trusses.

All structural members deployed by Hong Tet Engineering are sourced with accredited mill test certificates, typically conforming to **EN 10025 Grade S355JR** or equivalent high-tensile standards.

---

## 2. In-Workshop Pre-Fabrication vs. On-Site Erection

One of the cornerstones of successful site execution in land-scarce Singapore is maximizing **workshop pre-fabrication**:

1. **Computerized Band-Saw Cutting:** Precision cutting within ±1 mm ensures flawless joint alignment.
2. **Pre-Drilled Bolt Connections:** Fast bolt assembly on-site drastically cuts crane rental hours and reduces road marshal permit requirements.
3. **Certified Full-Penetration Welds:** Welded under controlled factory conditions by BCA-certified 3G/4G/6G structural welders.
4. **Protective Surface Treatment:** Hot-dip galvanization and high-solids zinc-phosphate epoxy primers applied before site delivery to counter tropical humidity.

---

## 3. On-Site Rigging, Lifting, and BCA Safety Protocols

During the lifting and erection stage, site safety is non-negotiable. Every structural lift requires:

- A dedicated **BCA-certified lifting supervisor** and certified signalman.
- Verified ground bearing pressure calculations for mobile crane outriggers.
- Daily inspection of web slings, bow shackles, and taglines.
- Secure tag-line guidance to prevent beam swing in tight industrial compounds.

> **Engineer's Tip:** Always ensure that structural bolt torques are verified with calibrated torque wrenches and marked with tamper-evident inspection paint before floor decking or concrete casting begins.

---

## 4. Key Handover & Verification Checklist

Before final signoff and tenant handover, Hong Tet Engineering conducts a comprehensive joint inspection covering:

- Verification of vertical column plumbness using precision optical instruments.
- Magnetic particle testing (MPI) or ultrasonic testing (UT) on critical splice welds.
- Protective paint touch-ups on all bolt heads and connection plates.
- Complete documentation handover including material mill certificates and certified fabrication drawings.
