# Tropical Weatherproofing: Zinc Sheet Roofing & Skylight Solutions

Singapore’s equatorial climate exposes industrial and commercial roofs to intense ultraviolet radiation, torrential downpours, and severe thermal expansion cycles. 

Without appropriate material selection and detail engineering, commercial roofs can quickly suffer from fastener loosening, joint degradation, and persistent rainwater leaks.

At **HONG TET ENGINEERING (S) PTE. LTD.**, we engineer industrial roofing and architectural canopy systems designed to withstand heavy tropical weather year after year.

---

## 1. Why High-Tensile Zinc Roofing Excels in Singapore

For warehouses, covered linkways, and factory roofs, corrugated zinc and aluminum-zinc coated steel sheets (such as Zincalume / Colorbond) remain the premier industry standard:

- **Superior Corrosion Resistance:** Aluminum-zinc alloy coatings form a self-healing barrier against coastal atmospheric salinity.
- **High Strength-to-Weight Ratio:** Enables wider purlin spacing without compromising roof load-bearing capacity under high winds.
- **Thermal Efficiency:** Heat-reflective coatings significantly reduce warehouse internal temperatures, reducing mechanical ventilation electricity bills.

---

## 2. Incorporating Natural Daylight: Polycarbonate Skylights

Integrating translucent skylights into industrial roofs drastically lowers artificial lighting requirements during daytime operational hours:

1. **UV Protective Co-Extrusion:** Multi-wall polycarbonate panels feature a microscopic co-extruded UV inhibitor that prevents yellowing, hazing, and embrittlement.
2. **Impact Resistance:** Withstands severe impacts from fallen tree branches and flying debris during intense storms.
3. **Thermal Expansion Accommodation:** Engineered with slotted screw holes and oversized neoprene washers to allow silent thermal expansion without cracking.

---

## 3. Critical Installation Details to Prevent Leaks

Over 85% of roof failures in Singapore stem from incorrect installation practices at laps and penetrations:

- **Minimum Slope Adherence:** Maintaining adequate roof fall ensures rapid rainwater evacuation without ponding.
- **EPDM-Capped Self-Drilling Fasteners:** Fasteners must be driven perpendicular to the purlin with exact torque to compress the EPDM seal without tearing it.
- **Continuous Ridge Cappings and Flashing:** Custom-folded galvanized flashing tailored on site to seal gaps around air vents and structural columns.

> **Maintenance Recommendation:** Schedule bi-annual gutter cleaning and roof fastener inspections right before the Northeast Monsoon season (November to January).
