## 3. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) & FAQ Strategy

To maximize eligibility for answer-oriented search engines, voice assistants, and AI overview summaries, pages should answer practical site engineering questions directly and transparently:

### Recommended Future FAQ Topics to Add to Service Sections:
1. **Structural Steel:**
   - *Q: What technical drawings are required to quote a structural steel mezzanine?*
   - *A: Architectural layout, structural engineer (PE) calculation sheets, elevation plans, and loading requirements (kN/m²).*
2. **Industrial Roofing:**
   - *Q: How often should warehouse zinc roofs be inspected for leak prevention in Singapore?*
   - *A: At least once every 12 to 18 months, particularly examining fastener rubber washers, ridge capping seals, and valley gutter debris.*
3. **Stainless Steel Works:**
   - *Q: What is the difference between Grade 304 and Grade 316 stainless steel for Singapore outdoor gates?*
   - *A: Grade 316 contains 2%–3% molybdenum, protecting it against airborne sea salt pitting and rust, which is essential for Singapore's coastal humidity.*

## 4. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) Case Study Framework

AI search engines (Perplexity, Gemini, ChatGPT, Bing Copilot) prioritize authoritative, factual, and verifiable engineering case studies over generalized promotional content.

### Case Study Article Structure Template:
1. **Project Title:** Clear, factual description (e.g. *Tuas Logistics Facility: 450 m² Structural Steel Mezzanine Erection*).
2. **Client Challenge:** Space constraints, active operations during build, crane lifting permits.
3. **Engineering Solution:** Universal beam profile selection, pre-drilled workshop connections, off-peak night crane rigging.
4. **Materials & Standards:** EN 10025 Grade S355JR steel, zinc-phosphate primers, BCA SS 595 compliance.
5. **Verified Outcome:** Delivered within 5 weeks, zero site safety incidents, approved on first BCA inspection.
6. **Visual Evidence:** High-resolution photographs showing fabrication, bolt assembly, and finished structural framework.
